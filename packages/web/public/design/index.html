Love Letter

Design direction, working document. The cards are the paintings; the interface is the gallery wall.

Principles

  • Only cards get ornament. Frames, gold, lapis, checker bands live inside the cards. The interface never borrows them.
  • No containers. Structure comes from hairlines, spacing and type size. No bordered panels, badges, pills or roundels in the chrome.
  • One accent, two jobs. Vermilion marks whose turn it is and the one button that commits a move. Nothing else is colored.
  • Everything is a sentence. The stage narrates the last action; the command area is a sentence you complete. It is the same sentence a terminal player types.
  • Numbers, not icons. Scores are counts next to names. Discard history is a string of numerals.

Geometry

  • Radius 4px on buttons, 2–3px on anything smaller. Cards keep their own, larger radius.
  • Hairlines are 1px and one color. Dividers, not boxes.
  • Shadows only under cards. Panels differentiate by spacing, not elevation.
  • Emphasis is a 2px underline in ink. Open choices are a dashed underline in ink 3. Disabled choices are struck through.
  • Three buttons exist: ink (navigation, start), accent (commit a move), outlined (secondary). Links are underlined text.

Motion

  • Cards deal into your hand from the deck's direction (500 ms, ease-out with a small settle), the second and third card 80 ms apart.
  • The card you play lifts away (350 ms). The stage sentence rises 120 ms later, the result line at 300 ms; a revealed card flips in 220 ms after the played one.
  • Ledger rows deal their cards and rise their text the same way, once, when they appear.
  • Status words slide in. Scores bump when they change. Overlays rise.
  • Nothing loops, nothing is decorative. All of it is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

Open questions

  • Phone: seats stack, stage cards shrink to 72px, hand goes full width, the ledger drops its sequence numbers. Still the least-tested surface.
  • Sound: none yet. If ever, one paper sound for the deal and nothing else.
